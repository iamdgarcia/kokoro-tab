How it works

  • 82M parameters — Kokoro is a frontier-quality TTS at tiny scale.
  • ONNX q8 → WASM — ~86 MB quantized model via ONNX Runtime Web, executed on your CPU.
  • Everything local — text is phonemized and synthesized in-tab; audio never leaves your machine.
  • 24 kHz WAV — generated as float samples, played straight from a blob URL.
